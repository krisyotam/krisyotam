## Sending patches

SourceHut is mailing-list-first and I keep it that way. If you want to contribute to anything under `~krisyotam`:

- Patches go to the project's list, not as PRs on a mirror. Use `git send-email` or `git-publish`.
- Sign your commits. My signing keys are at [krisyotam.com/pgp](https://krisyotam.com/pgp).
- One logical change per patch. If you have to explain it in the cover letter, split it.
- I read every patch. I do not always reply fast. Ping the list again after a week if I've gone quiet.

For anything sensitive (security reports, zero-days, anything you don't want indexed), email me directly and encrypt it.
